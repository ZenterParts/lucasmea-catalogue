Condition and country of origin

Each Lucas part number is one product with one set of fitting specifications. It can be offered new or remanufactured, and from more than one country of origin, so you can choose the option that suits your price and quality needs.

Technical data

FieldMeaning
Lucas part numberLRAG followed by four digits for alternators, LRCG for alternator components. One number per product, whoever makes it.
OriginCountry where the part is made: CN China, MY Malaysia.
VoltageCharging voltage as listed by the maker. 14 V and 12 V units both suit 12 V vehicles; 24 V and 28 V units suit 24 V vehicles.
OutputMaximum charging current in amps. Fit the same output as the original, or higher.
PulleyPulley code as listed by the maker: the number is the groove count, and F marks a freewheel (overrunning) pulley. Compare with the old unit.
RotationCW clockwise or CCW counter-clockwise, viewed from the pulley end.
Terminals / regulatorTerminal and regulator markings on the plug (for example B+, IG, L, S, FR, LIN). The plug must match the vehicle harness.

Vehicle lists come from the manufacturer’s application data. Always check the OE number, pulley and plug against the old unit before ordering. Specifications may change without notice.
