Condition and country of origin

Technical data

FieldMeaning
Lucas part numberLRSG followed by four digits. One number per starter motor, whoever makes it.
OriginCountry where the unit is made: CN China, MY Malaysia.
VoltageSystem voltage: 12 V for cars and light commercial, 24 V for trucks and heavy equipment.
PowerRated output of the starter in kilowatts. Small differences between makers (for example 0.9 and 1.0 kW) are normal for the same application.
Pinion teethNumber of teeth on the drive pinion. It must match the flywheel or flexplate, so always check it against the old unit.
RotationCW clockwise or CCW counter-clockwise, viewed from the pinion (drive) end.
Type / codeManufacturer type reference where known, useful for matching the old unit.

Vehicle lists come from the manufacturer’s application data. Always check the OE number, pinion teeth and rotation against the old unit before ordering. Specifications may change without notice.
