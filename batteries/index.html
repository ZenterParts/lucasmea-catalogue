Technology and quality

Built for more starting power

Made in EU Girling batteries use the Full Frame Design (FFD) grid: three times more resistant to corrosion than other grid designs, with more starting power for today's vehicles.

  1. 1Plate group: optimised design to guarantee sealing.
  2. 2Negative plate: special activated carbon that increases charge acceptance.
  3. 3Negative FFD grid: full-frame, continuous cast, special PbCa alloy.
  4. 4Separator: low-resistance PE separator with a premium glass-mat layer.
  5. 5Positive plate: built to withstand cycling, for 20% extra power.
  6. 6Glass-fibre mat: special non-woven mat in AGM and EFB batteries.
  7. 7Positive FFD grid: punched, corrosion-resistant PbCaSn alloy.

EN performance markings

What the markings mean

Made in EU Girling batteries meet EN 50342-1 and EN 50342-6. Each model is tested and classified on these criteria.

Water consumption W1–W5

The less water lost in service, the better. W5 is premium technology.

Charge retention C1–C2

Holding stored energy when not in use. C2 is modern calcium technology.

Vibration V1–V4

Resistance to vibration damage. V4 is for extreme use and modern commercial vehicles.

Endurance E1–E4

Repeated charge and discharge to 50% depth (conventional batteries).

Micro-cycle M1–M3

Frequent restarts and shallow cycling (EFB and AGM). M3 is demanding start-stop and hybrid.

Layouts, terminals and hold-downs

How to read the specifications. Ah is the 20-hour capacity (C20); CCA is cold cranking amps to EN 50342-1 at −18 °C; sizes are overall length, width and height in mm, including terminals.
