About Lucas

Lucas is an internationally trusted and recognised brand, with strong, robust and well-engineered quality products. Lucas parts are distributed in world markets by specialist partners.

Lucas air filters keep dust and grit out of the engine and protect the mass air flow sensor. Every Lucas air filter ships in the Lucas green carton, labelled with its Lucas part number.
